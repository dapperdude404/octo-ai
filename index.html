      2026 watch

      Themes that industry write-ups keep pointing to this year. These are expectations, not guarantees.

      • Agents that act. Apps move from answering to doing multi-step work. Analysts also warn many agent projects will be cancelled by 2027, so start narrow.
      • Voice and vision by default. Talking, showing a camera or a document is becoming as normal as typing. Octo’s Voice and Face tools are small versions of this.
      • On-device AI. Smaller models run on your phone or laptop for speed and privacy.
      • Reasoning models. Models that work through problems step by step are showing up in everyday tools.
      • Interfaces built on demand. Apps draw charts, forms and dashboards for your question instead of a fixed screen.
      • Rules and trust. Expect more disclosure rules for AI-made content, which is why AI detection and clear editing matter.
